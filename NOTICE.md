# Third-party material in Helm

Helm itself is GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It ships the following
third-party artwork, which carries its own terms.

## Flag artwork — EmojiOne v2.2.7

`Sources/Modules/Layout/UI/Flags/*.png` are the regional-indicator emoji from
**EmojiOne v2.2.7**, resized from the project's 128 px PNGs and renamed from
their Unicode code points to the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 region they depict.

- Source: <https://github.com/joypixels/emojione/tree/v2.2.7>
- Copyright: © JoyPixels (formerly Emoji One)
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**
  — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

v2.2.7 is the last release whose artwork is CC BY 4.0; the project's later
artwork is under JoyPixels' own licence and is deliberately not used here.

Attribution appears inside the app, on Settings → About, as CC BY 4.0 requires.
